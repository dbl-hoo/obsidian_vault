About Our FlexiSpot Bed

### What are the dimensions of the Classic Bed?

Full:79.79"×58.75"×14.03"  
Underbed space: 10"H  
Queen:84.79"×64.77"×14.03"  
Underbed space: 10" H  
King:84.79"×80.38"×14.03"  
Underbed space: 10" H  
  
Classic Bed + Headboard has a height of 39" from the bottom of the legs to the top cof the Classic Headboard.

### How much space is under the Classic Bed?

The Classic Bed has a generous 10.2" of underbed space, allowing for smart underbed storage.

### Do I need a box spring?

We recommend against it. The slats are perfectly spaced at just under 4" apart tevenly hold up your mattress while providing optimum support and breathability.

### Can I purchase the Classic Bed without the Classic Headboard?

The Classic Bed (Frame Only) is available to purchase without the Classic Headboard.

### How strong is the Classic Bed?

Very strong. The Classic Bed is designed not to squeak or creak,and has a weight capacity of 1,500 lbs+.

### Can a bed frame be placed completely against a wall?

Our bed frame is designed with recessed legs to fit seamlessly against baseboards, allowing it to sit flush against the wall while keeping the headboard stable.
