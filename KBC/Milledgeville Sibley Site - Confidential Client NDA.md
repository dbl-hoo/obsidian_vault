## Notes

2026-06-16 - NDA review; counter-markup prepared for Development Authority of the City of Milledgeville & Baldwin County
Stephanie Storm (KBC broker) forwarded a counter-executed NDA from the Authority (signed by Jonathan Jackson, Executive Director). Bill Breiner (LandMart, local broker) transmitted. Authority made four substantive changes to KBC's standard one-way NDA:

- **§1** — Added GORA definition. Accepted.
- **§3** — Added blanket GORA carve-out eliminating the notice requirement. Key problem: as drafted, Authority could respond to any GORA request without notifying the client. Countered with language that (a) acknowledges GORA compliance is permitted but (b) requires 3-business-day notice and cooperation with exemption efforts before disclosure. Discussed alternative: expand §3 trigger to "orders of governmental entities…including obligations under the Georgia Open Records Act" — cleaner, shorter, preserves notice machinery. Client to confirm preferred approach.
- **§4** — Retention carve-out for archival/regulatory/GORA purposes. Accepted; fixed typo ("copies of Confidential Information").
- **§6** — Fee-shifting: client reimburses Authority's costs if client requests Authority fight a GORA request. Accepted.
- **§7** — Survival period cut from 5 → 3 years. Countered at 4 years.
- **§8** — Governing law changed Delaware → Georgia. Accepted.

Also advised Stephanie on GORA disclosure risk generally and recommended client label all submitted materials as confidential/exempt under O.C.G.A. §§ 50-18-72(a)(34) and (36).
