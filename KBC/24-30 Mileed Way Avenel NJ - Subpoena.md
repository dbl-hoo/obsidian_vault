Background per Kontely:
- Parts Authority claims it became the commercial tenant at 24-30 Mileed Way by purchasing prior tenant Coney Island Auto Parts (purchase allegedly included a lease transfer). Mileed (LL) counterclaimed for lack of required consent to the assignment.
- Underlying case settled; Parts Authority stayed on as tenant. Aug 2023: Mileed noticed necessary repair work; Parts Authority vacated. Mileed did the work and now seeks to vacate the settlement and recover ~$200K. Parts Authority says it surrendered in proper condition and Mileed's repairs were capital improvements outside the lease.
- In motion practice, Parts Authority suggested KBC and Michael Kimmel have information on the tax treatment of Mileed's work (i.e., capital improvement) and the condition of the property — hence the subpoena to KBC.
- Kontely asks what more is needed to assist in responding to the request for additional information under the subpoena.
