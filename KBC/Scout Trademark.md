## Product Description

Scout is KBC's site selection and market analysis platform, designed to help brokers and clients identify opportunities, analyze data, and present insights in one place. Built on top of KBC's Bedrock database, Scout integrates property information, lease and sale comps, zoning layers, labor analytics, demographics, infrastructure, and site plans into a single interactive map and project workspace.

**Core capabilities:**
- **Identify** — Search, filter, and visualize properties and comps across markets
- **Analyze** — Layer in zoning, labor, demographics, and infrastructure data to assess site potential
- **Present** — Build and export market surveys, site plans, and project reports for clients

Scalable, self-service, designed to grow with KBC.
