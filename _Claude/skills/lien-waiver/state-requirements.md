# Lien Waiver — State-Specific Requirements

Each state section covers:
1. **Lien statute** — cite in Section 1 of the waiver
2. **Waiver language** — override for Section 1 body (leave blank to use standard template text)
3. **Notary form** — full acknowledgment block to paste in
4. **Special requirements** — anything else to flag or include

## Ohio

**Lien statute:**
Ohio Revised Code §§ 1311.85–1311.93 (Commercial Real Estate Broker's Lien Act)

**Section 1 cite:**
> including any broker's lien under Ohio Revised Code § 1311.85 et seq.

**Waiver language override:** *(none — use standard template)*

**Notary form:**
```
STATE OF OHIO
COUNTY OF _______________

Before me, a Notary Public in and for said County and State, personally appeared ___________________________, the ___________________________ of [KBC ENTITY], [entity type], known to me to be the person who executed the foregoing instrument, and acknowledged that he/she executed the same as his/her free act and deed on behalf of said limited liability company.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand and official seal this _____ day of _______________, [YEAR].

________________________________
Notary Public
My Commission Expires: ___________
```

**Special requirements:** None. Ohio does not require the waiver to be in a specific statutory form for broker's liens.

## States Without a Commercial Broker Lien Statute

The following states where KBC operates do **not** have a standalone commercial real estate broker lien statute, or broker lien rights are significantly limited. Use `[STATE STATUTE — VERIFY]` as a placeholder and flag for Jason before finalizing:

| State | Notes |
|---|---|
| California | No commercial broker lien act. General mechanics' lien law (Civil Code § 8000 et seq.) may apply in limited circumstances — verify with local counsel. |
| New York | No commercial broker lien statute. Commission claims pursued via contract. Omit lien statute cite; replace Section 1 with: "Broker waives and releases any and all claims, rights, and encumbrances against the Property arising from or related to Broker's brokerage services in connection with the sale of the Property." |
| Florida | Chapter 713 (construction liens) does not extend to broker commissions. Same substitute language as New York. |
| Arizona | No commercial broker lien statute — verify with local counsel. |
