# Commission Billing Requirements

What Kelly needs to bill a commission. Assemble these before submitting.

## Required for Every Deal

- Fully executed Renewal Amendment / New Lease / PSA
- Fully executed Commission Agreement
- Commission Calc
- PDF of CAR approval

## Required If Applicable

- Fully executed Co-Broker Agreement *(if co-broker involved)*
- Development Agreement *(if available)*

## Ohio Only

- Agency Disclosure — Jason creates this. **Does not need client signature** — just needs to be sent to the client and documented that it was sent.
