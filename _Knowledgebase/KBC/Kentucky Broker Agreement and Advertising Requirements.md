# Kentucky — Written Agency Agreement Contents & Advertising Requirements for Real Estate Brokers

**Short answer:** KY listing/agency agreements must be written and hit a fixed checklist of items under 201 KAR 11:121 (price, dates, broker/company name, fee, property description, signatures, showing restrictions, modification log); a baseline set of broker services is implied unless the client waives them in writing. Every ad must show the licensed brokerage's full name (or the principal broker's name with "principal broker" designated) — KRS 324.117 and 201 KAR 11:105 — with specific rules for internet/social ads and team/assumed names. Relevant to KBC's upcoming KY office (see [[Out-of-State Commission Paths]] — KY previously covered there only as a commercial cooperation state for non-licensed KBC; this file covers the rules that apply once KBC is directly licensed).

## Written Agency/Listing Agreement Requirements — 201 KAR 11:121

**Mandatory contents of a listing contract (Section 3):**

- (a) Listing price of the property, unless sale is by auction
- (b) Date and time of signing, for all parties who sign
- (c) Principal broker's full name and the real estate company name
- (d) Effective date of the listing and, if different, the date advertising may begin
- (e) Expiration date of the listing period
- (f) Fee, compensation, or other valuable consideration agreed between principal broker and client
- (g) Property address or a description sufficient to identify the parcel(s)
- (h) Signatures and printed names of authorized parties (including consideration of dower/curtesy rights)
- (i) Special directions from the client limiting/restricting showings
- (j) Date, time, and initials documenting any contract modifications made before acceptance

**Baseline services owed to the client (Section 2)** — unless the client waives them in writing:
- Accept and promptly submit all written offers
- Accept earnest money deposits
- Assist with offer development, negotiation, and presentation
- Answer client questions about terms and contingencies

## Advertising — KRS 324.117 and 201 KAR 11:105

- **No false, misleading, or deceptive advertising** — the umbrella rule under KRS 324.117.
- **Firm/broker identification required on every ad:** the full name of the real estate brokerage company registered with KREC, **or** the full name of the principal broker registered with KREC with a clear designation of principal-broker status. Required content must be clear and visible to a typical observer, in written text.
- **Listed-property ads:** whenever property is listed, every advertisement of it must include the company name on the listing licensee's license (or the principal broker's name) — regardless of who places the ad — unless the licensee is dealing in their own property. If a client/customer advertises the listed property themselves, the licensee must give them **written notice** of this requirement and keep a copy in the file.
- **Internet/social advertising:** each social media post or webpage is a separate advertisement. Required ID info must appear in the header/visible without scrolling, **or** via a clickable, clearly identifiable link to a page containing it.
- **Audiovisual ads:** broker identification required at the **start** of the ad (audible or written).
- **Consent required:** can't advertise property for sale/lease without the owner's written consent, and can't advertise another broker's listing without that broker's written consent.
- **Team/assumed names:** must be registered with KREC and must end in "Team" or "Group"; cannot imply the team/licensees offer brokerage services independent of the principal broker/company. Broader prohibition: no ad may lead a reasonable observer to believe brokerage services are being offered independently of the affiliated company/principal broker.
- **Firm-name-in-name provisions:** a deceased broker's name may remain in the firm name; an associate can be added to the firm name after 2 years' experience with the firm, averaging ≥20 hrs/week over 24 months.

## What This Means for KBC's New KY Office

- Build the KY listing/agency agreement template around the 201 KAR 11:121 Section 3 checklist — missing any element (price, dates, fee, description, signatures, showing restrictions, modification log) is a compliance gap, not just a drafting preference.
- Decide up front whether KY marketing runs under the full licensed KBC entity name or the principal broker's name — pick one and use it consistently on every ad, including social posts (link-out is fine only if the linked page carries the required info).
- If any KY broker/team wants a team brand, it needs KREC registration, must end in "Team"/"Group," and every ad still needs the actual firm/principal-broker name.
- No listed-property ad — including a client posting it themselves — can run without the required firm/broker attribution; if a client insists on advertising it themselves, document the written notice given to them.
