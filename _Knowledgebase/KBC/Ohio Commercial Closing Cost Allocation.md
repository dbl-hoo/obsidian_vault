# Typical Allocation of Closing Costs — Ohio Commercial / Raw Land Purchase

**Short answer:** Everything is contract-controlled in a commercial deal — no statute mandates allocation except the conveyance fee, which falls on the grantor (seller) by statute. The customary Ohio commercial split: seller pays conveyance fee, deed prep, lien releases, and commission; buyer pays recording, survey, lender costs, and (in most of Ohio) its own title work; taxes prorated with special attention to CAUV recoupment on farm land.

| Item | Typical payor | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Conveyance fee / transfer tax | **Seller** | State $1/$1,000 (ORC 319.54(G)(3)) + county permissive up to $3/$1,000 (ORC 322.02); county tax expressly on the grantor (ORC 322.06). Combined max 4 mills (0.4%). Most counties 3–4 mills total. |
| Owner's title insurance premium | **Regional custom** | Central/NE Ohio (Columbus, Cleveland): seller often pays owner's premium. SW Ohio (Cincinnati/Dayton): buyer customarily pays. Commercial deals frequently negotiate seller pays base owner's premium, buyer pays endorsements. |
| Title exam / search / commitment | Follows the premium | |
| Lender's policy + endorsements | **Buyer** | |
| Escrow / closing fee | **Split 50/50** | Common commercial default. |
| Deed preparation | **Seller** | Seller delivers limited warranty deed (commercial norm). |
| Recording — deed | **Buyer** | |
| Recording — mortgage releases / lien clears | **Seller** | Seller clears monetary liens. |
| New ALTA survey | **Buyer** | Seller typically delivers any existing survey as a DD document; buyer pays for its own new/updated ALTA. |
| Phase I / DD reports | **Buyer** | Seller delivers existing reports; buyer pays for its own. |
| Real estate taxes | **Prorated** | Ohio taxes paid in arrears — specify **short vs. long proration** in the PSA (long proration, covering the full arrearage, is the buyer-favorable ask and common custom in central/SW Ohio). |
| CAUV recoupment (raw farm land) | **Negotiate — flag every time** | Converting land out of agricultural use triggers recoupment of 3 prior years of CAUV tax savings (ORC 5713.34), which attaches as a lien. Custom is unsettled: buyer's development triggers it, so sellers push it to buyer; buyers argue it's seller's deferred tax. Allocate expressly in the PSA. |
| Broker commission | **Seller** | Per listing/commission agreement. |
| Attorney fees | Each party its own | |
