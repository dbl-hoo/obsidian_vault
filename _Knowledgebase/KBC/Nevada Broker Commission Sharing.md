# Can KBC (NV-licensed) Split a Commission with an Out-of-State Broker on an NV Deal?

**Short answer: Yes.** NRS 645.280(1) expressly states: "A licensed real estate broker may pay a commission to a licensed broker of another state." KBC, as the NV-licensed broker, can pay/split with the client's out-of-state broker — no cooperative certificate needed **just to split the fee**.

## The split, structurally

- KBC is the NV-licensed broker of record on the transaction; it collects the full commission and pays the out-of-state broker's share.
- This is the safe default: the out-of-state broker takes no active role in the NV transaction (no showings, negotiations, or licensed acts physically/functionally in NV) — pure referral/co-broke split, paid broker-to-broker.
- Paper it with a written referral/co-brokerage agreement (split %, triggering event, payment timing).

## If the out-of-state broker wants to actively work the deal in NV

If the client's broker wants to do more than just get paid — actually negotiate, show, or otherwise perform licensed acts on the NV side — that crosses into needing a **cooperative certificate**:

- **Basis:** NRS 645.605 (Administrator issues certificates authorizing an out-of-state licensed broker to cooperate with a Nevada broker) + NAC 645.180–.185 (application/use requirements).
- Only a **natural person** who is a broker qualifies (not an out-of-state brokerage entity) — must hold an active license in their home state, principal place of business there, and be in good standing.
- Certificate is **transaction-specific**: valid for the earlier of 12 months or completion/termination of the identified deal. Not transferable.
- The out-of-state broker must work **through** KBC (or a KBC-affiliated licensee) — KBC must remain in charge of the transaction start to finish.
- **All money in the cooperative transaction is handled only by KBC** (the NV broker), per NRS 645.310 — the out-of-state broker cannot take funds directly.
- Without the certificate, an out-of-state broker performing licensed acts in NV (e.g., bringing their client in to view/negotiate) is unlicensed activity — a violation exposing both the out-of-state broker's certificate *and KBC's NV license* to fine, suspension, or revocation.

## Bottom line

- **Just splitting the fee** (out-of-state broker stays out of the NV-side work): no certificate needed — NRS 645.280(1) covers it directly. Use a written co-broke/referral agreement.
- **Out-of-state broker wants to actively work the NV side**: get the NRS 645.605 cooperative certificate first; KBC stays in control of the transaction and all funds flow through KBC.
