# Out-of-State Commission Paths — States Where KBC Is Not Licensed

How KBC can legally collect a commission or referral fee in the 27 states where it does **not** hold a license. KBC is licensed in: AL, AZ, CA, CO, CT, DE, FL, GA, IL, MD, MA, NC, NV, NJ, NY, OH, OR, PA, SC, TN, TX, VA, WA.

> **This is research to scope the paths — not a formal legal opinion.** Statute numbers, reciprocity lists, and commission rules change. Confirm current text and the relevant commission's cooperation-agreement form before structuring any single deal.

## The Three Mechanisms

1. **Referral / cooperative split through a locally licensed broker** — KBC does *no* licensed activity in-state; the local broker performs all in-state work and pays KBC its split. Almost universally allowed because KBC holds active home-state licenses. Safest, lowest-friction path.
2. **Commercial cooperation license / agreement** — purpose-built carve-out (ID, KS, KY, NC, NH, OK, WI, MS, MO, NM) letting an out-of-state *commercial* broker actually work the deal alongside a resident broker, via a filed cooperation agreement + consent to jurisdiction. Use when KBC needs boots on the ground.
3. **Full nonresident license** (often by reciprocity, since KBC is licensed in 22 states) — when KBC wants standalone authority.

**Who invoices:** Cleanest structure is the local broker collects the full commission and pays KBC its share under the cooperation/referral agreement. Direct KBC-to-client invoicing requires KBC to hold a nonresident or cooperative license in that state. Assume broker-to-broker payment unless KBC is licensed/cooperatively authorized there.

## State-by-State

| State | Recommended Path | Statutory / Reg. Basis | Caveats & Flags |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alaska** | Co-broke through an AK broker; AK broker collects and pays KBC its split. KBC performs no in-state licensed acts. | AS § 08.88.331; 12 AAC 64 | AK firm must keep KBC's license copy + written fee request 3 yrs. No in-state activity by KBC. |
| **Arkansas** | "Physical-location" state. Referral/co-broke through AR broker who does *all* in-state work; **or** AR nonresident license by reciprocity (AR lists CO, FL, GA, IA, KS, LA, MS, NE, NY, OH, OK, PA, SD, WA, WV — KBC qualifies via several). | Ark. Real Estate License Law, Ark. Code Ann. tit. 17 ch. 42 (nonresident provisions) | KBC may do **zero** licensed activity in-state without a license. Nonresident applicants file irrevocable consent to service. |
| **Hawaii** | Referral split — HI broker pays commission to KBC as an out-of-state licensed broker. Broker-to-broker payment only. | HRS § 467-14(14); HAR § 16-99-3(k) | KBC may conduct **no** negotiations in HI. |
| **Idaho** | Get a **cooperative license** (commercial-only) — lets KBC actively work the deal with an ID broker. | Idaho Code tit. 54 ch. 20; IREC cooperative-license rule | Commercial/non-ag only; per-transaction; requires consent to service, license history, E&O. Not for residential/ag. |
| **Indiana** | 🚩 License **by endorsement** (no reciprocity, but endorsement waives the 90-hr course) is the clean path; or referral through an IN broker. | Ind. Code § 25-34.1-3 (endorsement) | **Flag for local counsel** — IN's rules on compensating an out-of-state broker not licensed in IN / co-brokerage are not cleanly settled. Confirm before relying on a bare referral split. |
| **Iowa** | Referral/co-broke through an IA broker (no in-state activity by KBC); **or** nonresident license (exam waivable by reciprocity). | Iowa Code §§ 543B.21–.22; IAC 193E | KBC takes no part in listing/showing/negotiating in IA unless licensed there. |
| **Kansas** | **Broker cooperation agreement** — KBC may perform commercial services in KS without a KS license if property isn't single-family residential. | K.S.A. § 58-3035 (commercial cooperation) | Local broker files the cooperation agreement w/ the commission within 5 business days. Commercial only. Strong fit. |
| **Kentucky** | Out-of-state principal broker may engage in **commercial** brokerage cooperating with a KY broker; file notice of affiliation. KBC also has reciprocity (OH, FL, TN, IL). | KRS §§ 324.236, 324.237 | Commercial only. File the KRS 324.237 affiliation notice. Strong fit. |
| **Louisiana** | 🚩 Cooperation agreement w/ an LA broker who supervises (filed w/ LREC); **or** nonresident license by reciprocity (CO, GA, etc.). | La. | **Maine** | 🚩 Referral fee through a Maine agency (written disclosure required); to *perform* services KBC must affiliate with a ME-licensed agency. | 32 M.R.S. § 13201 et seq. (ch. 114) | **Flag** — no clean commercial co-op carve-out; ME generally requires affiliation with a ME agency to do more than a passive referral. Written disclosure of the referral relationship is mandatory. |
| **Michigan** | Referral/co-broke through a MI broker; KBC takes its split and conducts no in-state negotiation. | MCL §§ 339.2510, 339.2512 | No in-state negotiation by KBC. MI bars paying unlicensed persons — KBC's home-state license is what makes the split lawful. |
| **Minnesota** | Referral/co-broke through a MN broker (out-of-jurisdiction licensee exception); **or** nonresident license. | Minn. Stat. § 82.70 | Payment flows through the cooperating MN broker. No in-state licensed acts by KBC without a license. |
| **Mississippi** | **Cooperative agreement** w/ an MS broker — KBC works the deal under MS-broker supervision. | Miss. Code Ann. § 73-35-11 | MS broker files agreement within 10 days, supervises showings/negotiations, holds escrow, and is **liable for KBC's acts**; joint-prominence advertising required. |
| **Missouri** | **Commercial** brokerage agreement w/ a MO broker; KBC consents to MO jurisdiction & commission discipline + appoints commission as agent for service. | Mo. Rev. Stat. §§ 339.010, 339.150 | Commercial only for the out-of-state carve-out. Consent-to-jurisdiction package required. |
| **Montana** | Referral/co-broke through an MT broker; no in-state service by KBC. | Mont. Code Ann. § 37-51-306 | Nonresident transactions trigger consent to legal process. No in-state activity. |
| **Nebraska** | Nonresident license is easy and clean (lets KBC work); or referral through NE broker. | Neb. Rev. Stat. § 81-885.17 | Nonresident license needs cert. license copy, 3-hr NE-law class, FBI fingerprint. Obtaining it = consent to personal jurisdiction. |
| **New Hampshire** | **Written cooperative brokerage agreement** w/ an NH broker — covers co-brokerage, referral, or other cooperation on a commercial deal. | RSA § 331-A:22-a | Commercial only. Agreement must state compliance + compensation terms; commission rules govern form/duration. Strong fit. |
| **New Mexico** | Transaction-specific **written referral/co-brokerage agreement** w/ an NM qualifying broker; or nonresident license (reciprocity-friendly). | NMSA 1978 § 61-29-16.1 | "Foreign broker" must consent to service. Agreement must be transaction-specific (or a general network/referral contract). |
| **North Dakota** | 🚩 Nonresident license **by reciprocity via GA** (ND reciprocal only w/ MN, IA, GA); else nonresident license w/ ND state-exam portion. | N.D.C.C. ch. 43-23; N.D.A.C. 70-02-01-06 | **Flag** — narrow reciprocity. Confirm the GA–ND reciprocal agreement is current before relying on it. |
| **Oklahoma** | **Cooperative brokerage agreement** w/ an OK broker (no licensed activity in OK by KBC); or nonresident license to do the work. | Okla. Stat. tit. 59 § 858-306 | If KBC performs licensed acts in OK, the cooperative agreement is **not** enough — it must get the nonresident license. |
| **Rhode Island** | Referral fee from an RI broker; or nonresident license. | R.I. Gen. Laws § 5-20.5-10 | No in-state showings/inspections/negotiations; KBC's name off the P&S. Exam-waiver reciprocity if KBC ever licenses. |
| **South Dakota** | Nonresident license by reciprocity (SD is reciprocity-friendly); or co-broke/referral through SD broker. | SDCL §§ 36-21A-54, -55 | Transacting business = consent to personal jurisdiction. Confirm reciprocity terms for KBC's home states. |
| **Utah** | 🚩 Referral fee through a UT broker is the practical path; nonresident license requires UT-law prelicensing + state exam (no full reciprocity). | Utah Code § 61-2f-401; Utah Admin. Code R162-2f | **Flag** — no clean out-of-state commercial co-op/referral provision surfaced. Confirm UT's commission-sharing rule for out-of-state brokers before relying. |
| **Vermont** | 🚩 Referral through a VT broker; or nonresident license (needs 2 yrs prior licensure). | 26 V.S.A. ch. 41 (§ 2291 nonresident) | **Flag** — VT's specific rule on paying an out-of-state broker a referral/co-broke fee not confirmed. Verify w/ VT Commission/local counsel. |
| **West Virginia** | Nonresident license by reciprocity (WV recently dropped the in-state-office requirement); or co-broke/referral. | W.Va. Code § 30-40-17 (am. 2025 SB 747) | Recently liberalized — nonresident brokers w/ a home-state office need no WV office. Broker-license experience thresholds apply. |
| **Wisconsin** | **Cooperative agreement** w/ a WI firm under § 452.137 — KBC need not be WI-licensed but is treated as a licensee. | Wis. Stat. §§ 452.137, 452.19 | KBC must give the WI firm proof of good-standing home license; must comply w/ WI law as a licensee. Clean commercial fit. |
| **Wyoming** | Referral/co-broke through a WY broker (no in-state negotiation); or nonresident license by reciprocity/substantial equivalence. | W.S. § 33-28-110 | No in-state negotiation by KBC for the referral path. Nonresident license requires reciprocal agreement or substantial-equivalence showing. |

## Bottom Line for KBC

- **Default everywhere:** route the fee as a referral/co-broke split through a locally licensed broker who does the in-state work and pays KBC. KBC's existing home-state licenses are what make this lawful in essentially all 27.
- **When KBC needs to actively work the deal:** cleanest commercial carve-outs are **KS, KY, NH, WI, ID, MS, MO, NM, OK** — just need a cooperation agreement (+ filing) with a resident broker.
- **North Carolina** — KBC is now directly licensed (2026-07-23); it's no longer in the non-licensed workaround list above. Direct licensed activity + direct invoicing available like any home-state jurisdiction.
- **Get local counsel before proceeding** in the flagged states: **Indiana, Louisiana, Maine, North Dakota, Utah, Vermont** — either the out-of-state-compensation rule is unsettled (IN, UT, VT), reciprocity is narrow (ND), or there's no clean commercial co-op path and licensure/affiliation is effectively required (ME, LA).
