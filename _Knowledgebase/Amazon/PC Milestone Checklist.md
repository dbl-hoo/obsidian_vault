# PC Milestone Checklist

Amazon/KBC **Project Coordinator** process bible — the step-by-step action checklist a PC runs from market survey through LOI, plus DD ownership, milestone definitions, WorkDocs filing structure, BU contacts, and vendor SLAs.

Source workbook: `_Knowledgebase/Amazon/PC Milestone Checklist 09.03.2026.xlsx` (rev 09.03.2026). 8 tabs.

## 1. Workflow Stages

Two parallel checklists, same skeleton, different detail: **First & Middle Mile** and **AMZL & RSR & SSD**.

| Stage | First & Middle Mile | AMZL / RSR / SSD |
|---|---|---|
| Market Survey — prep (w/ local broker) | ✔ | ✔ |
| Market Survey — prep (no local broker) | ✔ | ✔ |
| Market Survey — review | ✔ | ✔ |
| Site Tour | ✔ | ✔ |
| **Debrief** | — (First Mile not tracked) | ✔ |
| Phase 0 DD | Post Survey Review | Post Debrief |
| Pre-Site Selection | SEW prep (First Mile) | SEW (SSD) / DM+XM inputs (AMZL, RSR) |
| Site Selection | Monthly NACF approval (2nd Thursday) | Approvals Tool, PM notifies |
| Phase 1 DD | Post Site Selection | Post Site Selection |
| LOI | ✔ | ✔ |

**Key structural difference:** First/Middle Mile kick Phase 0 DD (labor + flash WHS) right after *survey review*. AMZL/RSR/SSD wait for the *debrief*, then fire a much bigger Phase 0 batch (flash WHS, traffic, zoning, power, crime, nat haz, ER, test fit).

### Market survey prep — with local broker
- Confirm survey is in proper template; KMZ meets KBC standards and **mirrors the excel survey**
- Fill gaps from (1) ReThink flyers, then (2) CoStar
- Confirm comps are in proper template
- First/Middle Mile only: add prior flash WHS from Phase 0 DD Tracker; add prior labor from HR Labor Analysis Master Tracker 2026
- Save survey + KMZ + flyers + comps to **Pre-Site Selection** folder in WorkDocs
- Schedule survey review w/ local broker, KBC broker, DM
  - First Mile: cc tdjone@, nicolrz@
  - Middle Mile: cc donflan@, flomacke@
- Remind broker to initiate **subagency agreement**

### Market survey prep — no local broker
- Export properties meeting the BRS from **Scout** (AMZL/RSR/SSD: CoStar *and* Scout)
- Fill gaps from ReThink flyers → CoStar
- Confirm w/ broker which exported sites make the survey
- Build survey in template + KMZ mirroring it
- Circulate to broker for pricing estimates & comments
- Export comps from Scout (and CoStar for AMZL/RSR/SSD); confirm parameters w/ broker
- Save all to Pre-Site Selection; schedule review

### Survey review
- Take notes in the **"Survey Review Notes"** section — follow-ups, review date, which sites shortlisted; save updated survey to WDs
- Circulate notes to DM, KBC broker, Broker Lead/PM by email
- Send survey + notes + KMZ to **data@** to update ReThink and LENS
- **Actualize Market Survey Date in Shield**

### Site Tour
- Tour books: direction comes from the Program Manager
- Save final tour book to Pre-Site Selection
- Actualize Site Tour Date in Shield
- AMZL/RSR/SSD: if no tour planned, clear the forecasted date in Shield and set risk to N/A

### Debrief (AMZL / RSR / SSD only)
- Confirm DM added the site to the debrief schedule; notify PM files are saved
- Create Pre-Site Selection subfolder named `Debrief [MM.DD.YYYY]`
- Trim the debrief survey to only sites being presented; confirm pricing included
- Build debrief KMZ per Debrief KMZ Training Guide
- SSD only: obtain site plans / CADs from landlord, save to WDs
- Attend the call, ready to drive the presentation (coordinate screen share w/ broker)
- Actualize debrief in Shield

### Phase 0 DD
**First & Middle Mile** (post survey review): request labor via aleseife@; request flash WHS in LENS; log addresses in the Phase 0 DD Tracker. Then on receipt: add results to survey, re-circulate to DM + broker, save flash WHS to Due Diligence folder w/ per-site subfolders, hyperlink in tracker, create LL Due Diligence subfolder, request test fit as DM directs, overlay test fit. Flash Power / Crime / Nat Haz / ER auto-generate off LENS.

**AMZL / RSR / SSD** (post debrief, after PM notes): test fit (Pre-Con for AMZL/RSR, XMs for SSD); flash WHS in LENS; flash traffic from KH; flash zoning; Flash Power / Crime / Nat Haz / ER auto via LENS; request existing DD from LL/owner (save in LL Due Diligence subfolder); anything else the debrief notes call for. Save everything to DD folder and update Shield.

### Pre-Site Selection deliverables
- **SEW document** (First Mile and SSD) — per SEW Document Guide + template
- Lease scenario cash flow (not required for land purchases) — confirm terms against latest LOI/broker
- Comps normalized and DM-approved; include comps map (SEW guide slides 13–14)
- First Mile extras: reasons-for-passing tab on alternative sites (use template dropdown); ROM estimate from PM; save 3–4 market reports incl. one CoStar to a Market Reports subfolder
- AMZL & RSR: inputs as requested by DM & XM

### Site Selection
Sites are selected in the **Approvals Tool**; PM notifies the project group. Migrate materials out of Pre-Site Selection per the WDs Organization structure.

### Phase 1 DD (post site selection)
Ordered with DM alignment: Short Form Noise (leases only; developer orders on purchases) · Power Assessment · Title (leases only; outside counsel on purchases) · Full Zoning · Phase I Environmental · **As needed:** Phase II, Long Form Noise, Full Traffic Study (TIA) · **PCR** for existing buildings >10 years old.

First/Middle Mile also: build selected-site KMZ with parcel info; push flash WHS + labor results into Shield.

### LOI
- Draft LOI as broker requests (**LOIs go out after debrief**, as directed by DM and broker)
- Save every iteration to WDs; final LOI to the Selected Site folder
- Send final LOI to **data@** to update ReThink
- Enter purchase price, SF/acreage, year built (existing only) into Shield
- **Actualize the LOI date in Shield**

## 3. RE Milestone Definitions

| Milestone | Definition |
|---|---|
| Market Survey Date | Date the longlisted survey is reviewed with the KBC broker and DM. (Same all BUs.) |
| Site Tour Date | First/Middle Mile: date the **DM** toured the MSA. SSD/AMZL/RSR: date the **DM and broker** toured the MSA. |
| Debrief Date | First Mile: **N/A, not tracked.** Middle Mile: date KBC shortlisted sites are presented to the business. SSD: date of most recent presentation of shortlisted opportunities. AMZL/RSR: date the most recent DM-reviewed sites are presented to the business. |
| **Site Selected** | ⚠️ **PC may only REFORECAST — the Program Manager owns actualizing this date.** First Mile: formal GCF site selection approval; NACF site selection is monthly, 2nd Thursday (subject to change). Middle Mile: formal site selection review (sites selected live in the meeting) or full stakeholder approval via the approval tool, depending on facility type. SSD: full approval by all required approvers in the Approvals Tool. AMZL/RSR: full stakeholder approval + formal approval sent through the `last-mile-approvals` Slack channel. |
| LOI Date | Date LOI terms are agreed to. |
| PSA Execution | Date the Purchase & Sale Agreement was fully executed. |
| Closing Date | Date Amazon officially closes on the property. |
| RE CAR Inputs | Date the RE CAR inputs were sent to Capital Planning **by the DM**. |
| RE CAR Approval | Date the CAR was fully approved in the approval tool. |
| ES Submitted Date | Date the Executive Summary is launched in the approval tool **by legal**. |
| M10 Lease/Contract Approval | Date the lease was fully executed. |
| Development Agreement Date | Date the Development Agreement was fully executed. |

| Last Mile | AMZL | `W:\...\NON CORE\Last Mile\AMZL` |

### Subfolder contents

**Survey / Pre-Site Selection** — survey (xlsx + KMZ), flyers, comps, a Debrief folder (debrief KMZ, debrief survey, flyers, topology heatmap/table; PM moves debrief notes post-debrief), shortlisted-site subfolders (LOIs, test fits/site plans, CADs, KMZ outline), proposal comparison if applicable.
Naming: `Address, City, State` — **AMZL: `Site Code - Address, City, State`**

**Due Diligence** — pre-selection: per-shortlisted-site subfolders with flash WHS, flash power, crime, nat haz, ER, and a "LL Provided DD" folder. At site selection: move all subfolders into a new folder titled **`xLonglist`**, and keep as loose files — title documents (leases), power study, full zoning report, Phase I, short form noise, flash traffic, plus Phase II / long-term noise / TIA / PCR / ALTA if applicable. Also WHS approval, tax approvals, final zoning memo.

**Selected Site** — SSD only pre-selection: the SEW launched in the approval tool. Post-selection, migrate for the selected site only: LOIs (named w/ site code + execution date), KMZ outline with parcel ID, site selection materials (First Mile; PM moves), early access agreement, CRA, an RFQ subfolder (developer responses, comparison, lead/DM approval email), and final executed deal docs managed by legal (lease, PSA, buyer settlement statement, development agreement + work order for BTS).

**Drawings** — test fits, CADs, migrated for the selected site only.

**CAR** — final cash flow with FMV, final comps, CAR white paper (last mile only), Page 0 narrative (RELO only), CAR approval.

**BaseCamp** — pre-selection: subagency / co-brokerage agreement. Post-selection: fully executed lease or PSA, commission agreement, commission calculation, FMV calculation, closing/settlement statements (purchases), and for BTS — dev fee agreement, CAR approval, GMP budget.

**Approvals** and **Brokerage** folders are slated for removal.
