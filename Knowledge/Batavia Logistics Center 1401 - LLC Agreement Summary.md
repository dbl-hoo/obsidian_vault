## Key Provisions

### Capital Calls (§7.2)
- Manager may call additional capital pro rata by Contribution Percentage.
- **10-day response window** from written request.
- No member has personal liability to contribute — failure triggers remedies below only.

### Non-Contribution Remedies
- **Default Loan (§7.2(b)(1)):** Any other member may advance the defaulting member's share. Terms: 18% per annum, compounded annually, unsecured. All distributions otherwise payable to non-contributing member are redirected to repay the loan until paid in full.
- **Third-party borrowing (§7.2(b)(2)):** Manager may borrow from third parties or take member advances (non-Capital Contribution debt) to cover shortfall.

### Distributions (§9.1)
- **Distributable Cash re: Capital Contributions** → Members pro rata by Percentage Interest
- **Distributable Cash re: Promotes and Fees** → KBC Member exclusively (0% capital, 100% of promote)

### Management (§10)
- **Exclusively by Manager.** Non-Manager members cannot act for or bind the Company.
- **2/3 vote of Percentage Interests required** for: amending the agreement, amending the Project Partnership Agreement (if materially adverse), issuing equity, related-party transactions, certain acquisitions.
- Manager indemnified except for gross negligence, willful misconduct, fraud, or breach.

### Transfers (§15)
- Restricted — Manager consent required (sole and absolute discretion).
- Exceptions: transfers to Affiliates (if Affiliate status maintained); indirect transfers not constituting change of control.

### Confidentiality (§17.12)
- All terms, deal discussions, and financial info are confidential.
- Carve-outs: employees/advisors with need to know; required by law; litigation.

### Non-Disparagement (§17.13)
- Runs from Effective Date through **2 years after disposition of all property interests**.
- Covers all members and their affiliates.

### Counsel Conflict Note (§17.14)
- Munsch Hardt represents Company, KBC Member, and Manager.
- Non-KBC Members explicitly acknowledged they are unrepresented by Munsch Hardt and were encouraged to retain independent counsel.

## Notes for Capital Call Matter

- Default Loan remedy is the operative enforcement mechanism if a member fails to fund a capital call.
- Interest rate on default: **18% per annum, compounded annually**.
- Distributions are the primary repayment mechanism — withheld and redirected to Lending Member.
- 10-day cure window after written demand is critical to triggering the remedy.
- Jurisdiction is **Cook County, IL** (despite Texas governing law) — no jury trial.
