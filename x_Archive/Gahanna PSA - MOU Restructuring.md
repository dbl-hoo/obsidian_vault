### AGREEMENT

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the foregoing and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

#### 1. Existing Agreement in Full Force and Effect

The Agreement remains in full force and effect as of the date of this MOU. No termination of the Agreement shall occur unless and until all Conditions to Termination set forth in Section 3 below are simultaneously satisfied. Until such time, VRG and Velocis shall continue to perform their respective obligations under the Agreement.

---

#### 2. Proposed Transaction

VRG and Tenby are in early-stage, non-binding discussions regarding a potential Tenby PSA for the Property. The Parties acknowledge that:

(a) No binding agreement between VRG and Tenby exists as of the date of this MOU;

(b) No Party is obligated to enter into any termination or related transaction unless and until a definitive Tenby PSA is executed; and

(c) Any termination of the Agreement is conditioned solely upon satisfaction of the Conditions to Termination in Section 3.

---

#### 3. Conditions to Termination

The termination of the Agreement shall be conditioned upon the **simultaneous** occurrence of all of the following (the "Conditions to Termination"):

(a) VRG and Tenby executing a non-binding letter of intent with respect to the Property (the "Tenby LOI");

(b) VRG and Tenby executing a definitive Tenby PSA; and

(c) Tenby separately agreeing to purchase KBC's due diligence materials on terms mutually acceptable to Tenby and KBC (the "DD Sale"), either prior to or contemporaneously with execution of the Tenby PSA.

Until all Conditions to Termination are satisfied, the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect, and no Party shall take any action to terminate or rescind the Agreement.

---

#### 4. Compensation to KBC/Velocis Upon Termination

In consideration of the termination of the Agreement, KBC and/or Velocis shall receive the following compensation:

**(a) DD Sale Proceeds.**
Tenby shall pay KBC $[___________] for KBC's due diligence materials pursuant to the DD Sale. *[Issue #4 above — amount TBD; insert prior to circulation.]* Payment shall be made prior to or contemporaneously with the execution of the Tenby PSA.

**(b) Earnest Money Reserve.**
Upon execution of the Tenby PSA, VRG shall cause the title company or escrow agent to hold in escrow, for the benefit of KBC, an amount equal to the lesser of (i) the total earnest money deposit made by Tenby under the Tenby PSA and (ii) Seventy‑Five Thousand Dollars ($75,000) (the "Reserved Amount").

**(c) If the Tenby PSA Closes:**

(i) The Reserved Amount held in escrow shall be released to KBC at closing of the Tenby PSA; and

(ii) VRG shall pay KBC, from VRG's sale proceeds at closing, an additional cash payment (the "VRG Closing Payment") equal to:

> **$200,000 − Reserved Amount − DD Sale Proceeds**

so that KBC's total compensation from all sources equals $200,000.

*Illustrative Example:* $200,000 − $70,000 (Reserved Amount) − $70,000 (DD Sale Proceeds) = $60,000 VRG Closing Payment.

**(d) If the Tenby PSA Does Not Close:**

(i) The Reserved Amount held in escrow shall be released to KBC as promptly as practicable after termination of the Tenby PSA, **provided** that if Tenby is entitled to recover its earnest money under the Tenby PSA due to Tenby's default or otherwise, the Reserved Amount (or the applicable portion) shall be returned to Tenby and KBC shall have no claim thereto; *[Issue #6 above — consider guaranty from VRG.]*

(ii) VRG shall have no obligation to pay KBC the VRG Closing Payment or any other amount representing the balance of the $200,000; and

(iii) KBC's Re‑Contracting Rights under Section 5 shall apply.

---

#### 5. Re‑Contracting Rights

If the Tenby PSA terminates or Tenby fails to close, KBC's right to re‑contract with VRG shall be governed by the following, based on KBC's total compensation received:

**(a) Scenario A — KBC Received Less Than $200,000.**
If the total compensation received by KBC from all sources (DD Sale Proceeds + Released Reserved Amount) is less than $200,000, KBC shall have the right to re-contract with VRG to acquire the Property on the existing terms of the Agreement (as amended through the Fifth Amendment), subject to the following conditions:

(i) Re-contracting shall not require KBC to post additional hard earnest money;

(ii) The re-contracting period shall expire on December 31, 2026 (the "Re-Contract Deadline"); and

(iii) The Parties shall negotiate in good faith toward a new purchase and sale agreement within 15 days of KBC's written election to re-contract.

**(b) Scenario B — KBC Received $200,000 or More.**
If KBC's total compensation equals or exceeds $200,000, KBC shall have the right, but not the obligation, to negotiate in good faith with VRG toward a new purchase and sale agreement, on terms to be mutually agreed, through the Re-Contract Deadline.

**(c) Extension Right.**
In either Scenario A or B, if KBC presents VRG with a duly-executed (or materially negotiated) non-binding letter of intent from a bona fide third-party purchaser prior to the Re-Contract Deadline, KBC may extend the investigation/inspection period under any re-executed agreement in consecutive 30-day increments, for a maximum of three (3) extensions (90 days total), **without posting additional hard earnest money**. This right is intended to allow KBC sufficient time to seek re-approval of its development plan or to modify the development plan in accordance with the third party's requirements.

**(d) Extensions Beyond 90 Days.**
If KBC requires an investigation/inspection period extension beyond the 90-day cumulative limit described in Section 5(c), any further extension shall require KBC to post additional earnest money in amounts to be negotiated by VRG and KBC at such time, in good faith.

**(e) Forfeiture of Re-Contracting Rights.**
All re-contracting rights under this Section 5 expire absolutely on December 31, 2026 and shall not be extended except by written agreement of VRG and KBC. *[Issue #10 above — confirm this date against current FDP approval expiration.]*
