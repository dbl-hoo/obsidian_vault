Very challenging to get MCCD's sign-off on completion and also tough on inspections during the construction process.
4. Oil/Gas: $150,000 - This area is littered with old oil/gas lines and wells. We had to plug a gas well in the middle of our site and have a transmission line to the north that wasn't in an easement that we had to work through/get contained within an easement.
5. Landscaping: $350,000 – landscaping requirements here can be a little strict but we had to do a decent amount due to us fronting their main road.
6. Numbers above do not include any paving, concrete, fencing, lighting, etc. since I assume that won't be comparable to your project

**Offsites:** $735,000 to install a signal at our site entrance to Hermitage Road (PennDOT Route). PennDOT district here is generally good to work with but they can be slow on issuing the HOP and Hermitage was able to get comfortable releasing our site plan approval/grading permit at risk prior to us receiving.

**Power**: Just a note that there was a constraint on power in this area when we started construction. Not sure if you're close by and/or if the utility has done anything to improve that since we completed.
