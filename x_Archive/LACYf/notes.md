here’s an overview of the Southern New Jersey (SNJ) industrial market and our perspective on anticipated strike pricing:

- The SNJ market has softened recently, driving aggressive lease rates, particularly in the 50–250k SF size range — historically the most active segment with ~10 deals/year — but now facing nearly **two years of supply**.
- Larger size ranges (250–500k, 500–750k, 750k–1M, and 1M+ SF) are even more out of balance, with supply exceeding expected demand by over two years.
- The 750k–1M SF and 1M+ SF segments, while less oversupplied (each with just one availability), tend to see limited tenant demand.
- In lower Gloucester County, asking rents are typically around **$12.00/SF**, but recent strike prices have ranged from **$11.35 to $10.15/SF**, depending on size and location.
