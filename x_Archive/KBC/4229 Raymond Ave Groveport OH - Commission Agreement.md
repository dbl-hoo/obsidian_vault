2026-06-15 - Risk flag: agreement is structured w/ JLL (not MetLife/Landlord) as KBC's contracting counterparty ("Agent" in its own corporate capacity). Section 5 disclaims KBC as third-party beneficiary of JLL/Landlord agreement; Section 9 waives KBC's lien rights. Under Ohio's broker's lien statute (R.C. 1311.85-1311.86), a lien requires a commission agreement signed by the broker and the owner (or owner's agent acting to bind the owner) — since KBC's agreement is with JLL as principal, KBC likely wouldn't qualify for a lien here even absent the Section 9 waiver. Practical effect: KBC's commission claim runs against JLL only, contingent on JLL being paid by MetLife, with no lien fallback against the property. Drafted email to Beau Taggart and Kristina Madayag flagging this — noted it's standard practice for institutional owners (keeps owner out of broker disputes/lien exposure), not unusual to this deal, but worth having on record.
