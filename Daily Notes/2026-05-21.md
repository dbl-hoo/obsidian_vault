## [[WOH1]] - response from LL:  

• Maintain the rental rate at the current $7.00/SF for the renewal term  
• No free rent concession  
• We would be open to the proposed four (4) renewal terms, or we can maintain the existing structure of three (3) renewal options as already negotiated in the First Amendment  
• Maintain the current lease framework with minimal operational changes

There are several reasons why ownership believes this is the appropriate position.

First, Amazon’s proposal effectively attempts to reset economics backward. The current rate of $7.00/SF was already negotiated from a significantly lower prior rate of approximately $4.97/SF during the previous extension. Their request to reduce rent back down to $5.95/SF represents a material decrease in value despite continued inflationary pressures, increased operating costs, and stronger industrial market fundamentals compared to when the original extension was negotiated.

Second, the request for two months of free rent does not appear justified. This is not a new occupancy, expansion, or major reinvestment scenario. Amazon is an existing tenant with established operations already in place. From an ownership perspective, granting free rent in combination with a reduced rental rate creates an unnecessary concession package without a corresponding benefit to the landlord.

Third, the current lease already provides Amazon with broad transfer and assignment rights under Section 17(a). Legal’s review did not identify significant concerns there, and operationally we do not appear to be receiving meaningful additional value in exchange for the requested economic reductions.

In addition, under the existing lease language, Amazon’s renewal rights already contemplate rent being tied to 90% of fair market value. Based on current industrial market conditions and replacement costs, ownership does not believe $5.95/SF accurately reflects current market economics. Holding firm at $7.00/SF appears to be a reasonable and defensible position, especially considering:

• Inflation in construction and operating costs  
• Continued demand for functional industrial space  
• Rising replacement costs for similar facilities

Note:  we agreed to $7PSF for renewal from June 26 to May 27.  We then proposed $5.95 for a 3 year renewal with 2% bumps.
