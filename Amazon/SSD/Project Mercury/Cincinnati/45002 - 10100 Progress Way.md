## Final LOI (2026-10-15) — key terms

Dated October 15, 2026. **Seller-signed 10/15/26** by Jason R. Wittekind, President, Progress Way III LLC. Filed at `Documents\Amazon\Project Mercury\Cincinnati\SVG2 - 10100 Progress Way\LOI - Progress Way - Seller Executed 2026.10.15.pdf`.

- **Parties:** Buyer is Amazon.com Services LLC (DE). Seller is Progress Way III LLC.
- **Property:** ~13.7 ac, parcels 5610024001300 and 5610024001600 (Exhibit A aerial).
- **Price:** $165,000 / net acre (up from $150K in the 10/1 draft).
- **Earnest money:** $20K within 10 business days of the PSA effective date, held by Chicago Title (701 5th Ave, Suite 2700, Seattle). Refundable during the Inspection and Project Approvals Periods. Applies to the price at closing.
- **Inspection:** 120 days from the Effective Date, at Purchaser's cost. Covers title, zoning, soils, environmental, wetlands and utilities. Purchaser can terminate with the deposit returned if title or survey objections aren't resolved before closing.
- **Extensions:** two × 120 days. Each costs an extra $10K deposit within 10 days of the election, **non-refundable**. The initial $20K stays refundable through the extended period. All deposits apply to the price.
- **Approvals:** Purchaser gets plan approvals and confirmations during the Inspection Period.
- **Early access:** an early access agreement (Purchaser's form, with insurance and indemnity) gets signed during PSA negotiation.
- **Title and survey:** Purchaser pays for the title commitment and a new ALTA. Seller provides any existing ALTA.
- **Closing:** within 45 days after the Project Approvals Period (or its extension) expires. Limited warranty deed.
- **Costs:** split per market convention, each side pays its own attorneys.
- **Assignment:** to an affiliate, developer or capital partner without consent. Purchaser pays any transfer taxes caused by the assignment.
- **Commission:** 4% of the price, paid by Seller at closing to KBC/SqFt.
- **Exclusivity:** 30 days from the LOI date and through the PSA term (binding).
- **Confidentiality:** the separately executed NDA dated 2026-10-01 (binding).
- **Expiry:** the offer expired if not accepted within 5 business days. Seller accepted the same day.
- **Incentives Questionnaire:** attached (pp. 6–7) but **blank**. The seller hasn't returned it.
- **Drafting note:** the closing and earnest money terms still refer to a "Project Approvals Period", but the LOI no longer defines one. Approvals now fall inside the Inspection Period.
