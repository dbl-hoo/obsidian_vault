2026-07-09 - PSAs signed 7/8. Stream 8 potential impact analysis logged.

**PSAs executed 2026-07-08** (both parcels).

**Stream 8 impact — what it entails if site design fills/disturbs the intermittent reach (~403 LF, 5 ft wide ≈ 0.05 ac, eastern boundary):**

1. **New JD required first (needed regardless).** 2021 AJD expired 6/15/2026 and was issued under the vacated NWPR. Post-*Sackett*, an intermittent tributary with seasonal flow to Clear Creek → Great Miami almost certainly remains jurisdictional as a relatively permanent water. Re-delineation + new JD request to USACE Huntington District (prior file LRH-2021-416-GMR-UT Clear Creek). ~2–4 months.
2. **Section 404 — NWP 39 is the likely path.** Commercial/institutional development NWP covers up to ½ acre of WOTUS loss; ~0.05 ac is comfortably under. PCN to the Corps; verification typically 3–6 months. If it kicks to an individual permit (public notice, alternatives analysis): 12+ months — keep impacts tight to avoid.
3. **Ohio EPA 401 WQC threshold is the number to watch.** Ohio's regional conditions on the NWPs have historically triggered an *individual* 401 (public notice, antidegradation findings, longer timeline) above ~300 LF of intermittent/perennial stream impact. At 403 LF total, designing to impact **<~300 LF** likely keeps the whole process streamlined. Env consultant to verify current thresholds day one.
4. **Compensatory mitigation.** Stream credits, likely in-lieu fee (TNC — same program as IDI's wetland credits), debited per Huntington District/Ohio stream metrics. For ~400 LF of moderate-quality intermittent channel, ballpark low-to-mid five figures; permit condition.
5. **Design alternatives to price before permitting:** pull grading/detention back so only a culverted crossing touches the reach (much smaller impact number), or relocate the channel along the property line (permittable, but counts as impact + mitigation and draws more scrutiny).

Ohio EPA isolated wetland permit (expires 11/3/2026) is a separate track — does not cover Stream 8.
