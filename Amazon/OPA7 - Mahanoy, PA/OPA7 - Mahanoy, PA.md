Mountain Valley – Building 2 is an industrial development located at 2166 Burma Road in Barnesville, Pennsylvania. The 252-acre site was acquired by PNK Development from NorthPoint Development in December 2024 for $23,352,900 as a rough-graded, fully entitled pad. NorthPoint completed all entitlement work prior to the sale, and PNK began vertical construction in late summer 2025. Construction progress currently includes structural columns set and wall panel installation underway. Project delivery is targeted for Q3–Q4 2026.

Guidance rental rates are in the low- to mid-$8.00/SF range. Operating expenses are not yet finalized, but early estimates indicate $0.75–$1.00/SF, inclusive of the 10-year LERTA. The LERTA provides 100% tax abatement in years 1–8, 90% in year 9, and 80% in year 10.

The planned facility totals approximately 1,240,720 SF with a 40' clear height. The base site plan includes 218 dock-high doors, 4 drive-in doors, 323 trailer stalls, and 314 car parking spaces, with the potential to add roughly 210 additional car stalls along the east side. A secondary site configuration (attached) shows an alternative layout of 454 car stalls and 450 trailer stalls. The site offers full perimeter truck circulation.

All 218 dock positions are designed to include 40,000-lb mechanical levelers. The structure consists of concrete columns supporting a standard steel roof deck. Electrical capacity is currently planned at approximately 4,000 amps. The developer has not yet evaluated options for expanded service but believes additional power may be achievable pending a load study.

Although the overall parcel comprises 252 acres, significant portions are not developable due to topographic constraints. As a result, the building pad and associated parking fields occupy nearly all usable acreage, leaving limited opportunity for expansion or meaningful site plan modifications.

Access to the development is provided by a single private road that connects to Route 54 at a signalized intersection near the Interstate 81 (Exit 131) interchange. The distance from the site to the I-81 on-ramp is approximately 0.5 miles. This private road serves as the sole ingress and egress for the project.

Adjacent to the property is a 1,225,120-SF NorthPoint-developed building, of which approximately 667,539 SF remains available, with the balance leased to Box Partners.

Historically, the property operated as Bendinsky Airport, a private airfield built in the 1960s and closed in the 1980s. The current building pad generally aligns with the former runway footprints.

The site is presently under consideration as a potential backup candidate for RELO (AVPx).
