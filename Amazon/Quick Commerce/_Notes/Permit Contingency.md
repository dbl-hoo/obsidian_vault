
Permit Contingency. Tenant has entered into this Lease in the expectation of obtaining, after the expiration of all applicable appeal periods (and, if conditionally issued, having conditions acceptable to Tenant in its sole discretion), all approvals, permits, variances, special use permits, licenses, permissions and other authorizations required from Landlord and all governmental or quasi-governmental authorities and third parties (whether private or public) having approval authority pursuant to Legal Requirements, contract, or otherwise (“Permits”) necessary for the (a) operation of Tenant’s business on the Premises, including for the Permitted Use, and (b) construction and installation of any initial improvements and related improvements and Tenant’s property with respect to the Premises, including all signage. Tenant agrees to use reasonable efforts to apply for Permits without unreasonable delay. Landlord agrees to reasonably cooperate with Tenant in obtaining the Permits, including by executing applications, affidavits, consents, access permissions, or other documentation reasonably required by any governmental authority, and by promptly responding to related requests by Tenant, and Landlord shall not unreasonably delay, condition, or withhold any such cooperation. If Tenant is unable to obtain all Permits, Tenant may terminate this Lease by delivering 10 days’ written notice of such termination to Landlord at any time until Tenant has obtained all Permits.
